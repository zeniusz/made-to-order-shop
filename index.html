How to measure yourself
  1. Chest. Around the fullest part, under your arms, tape level. Your jacket size is about half of this in cm.
  2. Waist. Around the narrowest part, just above the navel.
  3. Sleeve. From the shoulder seam to the wrist bone, arm slightly bent.
  4. Jacket length. From the base of the collar to where you want the jacket to end.

Wear a thin shirt and ask someone to help. Keep the tape snug, not tight.
